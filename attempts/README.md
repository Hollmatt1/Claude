## crate_c_v1 — best yet

![crate_c_v1](crate_c_v1.png)

264 mm cube, 10 volumes, 12 parts. Exterior copies crate C: light corner posts,
deep top cap band, recessed field panels, latch plate, louver, placards.

Restore with `cp attempts/crate_c_v1.py src/manga_cube.py`.

Two things it did not solve, both of which turned out to be solvable:

- **Corner posts were thin (31 mm).** Capped by the pocket margin — but only on
  the *front* face, where the pocket actually opens. The side faces have no such
  limit, so the posts can be much deeper there.
- **Two-tone could not colour the posts.** True for a tier-based colour split,
  because the posts run the full height. Not true in general: the posts can be
  separate slide-on covers, trapped by the cap.
