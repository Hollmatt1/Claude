## Photo tips (retake before posting if you can)
Buyers respond best to clean, well-lit shots:
1. **Couch:** Take off the blankets, pillows, laptop, and bag. Photograph it straight on in daylight, plus one close-up of the fabric.
2. **Coffee table:** Clear the top completely and shoot from a low angle so the base shows. Add a close-up of the glass edge.
3. **KICKR:** Take the bike off, move it away from the boxes, and photograph it against a plain wall. Include a shot of the model label or flywheel.
